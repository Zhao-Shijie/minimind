Machine learning is a subset of artificial intelligence that enables systems to learn from data.
Deep learning uses neural networks with many layers to model complex patterns.
Natural language processing helps computers understand human language.
Transformers have revolutionized the field of NLP with their attention mechanisms.
Pre-training on large corpora allows models to learn rich language representations.
Fine-tuning adapts pre-trained models to specific downstream tasks.
The gradient descent algorithm optimizes neural network weights by minimizing loss.
Backpropagation computes gradients efficiently through the chain rule of calculus.
Regularization techniques like dropout and weight decay prevent overfitting.
Large language models can generate coherent text across many domains.
Tokenization splits text into smaller units for processing by neural networks.
Attention mechanisms allow models to focus on relevant parts of the input.
The softmax function converts raw scores into probability distributions.
Cross-entropy loss measures the difference between predicted and true distributions.
Adam optimizer combines momentum and adaptive learning rates for efficient training.
Layer normalization stabilizes training by normalizing activations across features.
Positional encodings inject order information into permutation-invariant self-attention.
The transformer architecture consists of stacked encoder and decoder layers.
Residual connections help gradients flow through very deep neural networks.
